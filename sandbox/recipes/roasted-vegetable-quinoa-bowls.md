# Roasted Vegetable Quinoa Bowls

These roasted vegetable quinoa bowls make a hearty, healthy, and delicious vegetarian meal. The combination of tender roasted veggies, fluffy quinoa, and tasty toppings is simply irresistible.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 medium sweet potato, cubed 
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 1 zucchini, chopped
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 2 tbsp toasted pumpkin seeds
- Chopped parsley for garnish

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss the sweet potato, bell pepper, zucchini, and onion with the olive oil on a baking sheet. Season with salt and pepper.
2. Roast the vegetables for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway, until tender and lightly browned.
3. Meanwhile, cook the quinoa: Combine the quinoa and vegetable broth in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 15-20 minutes, until quinoa is fluffy.
4. To assemble the bowls, divide the quinoa and roasted vegetables between 4 bowls. Top each with sliced avocado, crumbled feta, toasted pumpkin seeds, and chopped parsley.

Enjoy this nourishing and satisfying roasted vegetable quinoa bowl! The mix of textures and flavors is simply delightful.
