# Quick Homemade Ramen

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes
**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

### Broth:
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch piece ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp miso paste
- 1 tsp sesame oil

### Noodles & Toppings:
- 2 portions ramen noodles (fresh or dried)
- 2 soft-boiled eggs
- 1 cup sliced mushrooms
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 cup baby spinach
- Nori sheets
- Sesame seeds
- Chili oil (optional)

## Instructions
1. Bring broth to boil in a large pot
2. Add garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and miso paste
3. Simmer for 10 minutes to develop flavors
4. Add sesame oil
5. In separate pot, cook noodles according to package directions
6. Sauté mushrooms in a pan until golden
7. Soft-boil eggs (6-7 minutes), then peel and halve
8. Divide noodles between two bowls
9. Ladle hot broth over noodles
10. Top with eggs, mushrooms, spinach, green onions, and nori
11. Add sesame seeds and chili oil if desired

## Upgrades
- Add sliced pork belly or chicken
- Use dashi for more authentic flavor
- Make homemade broth from scratch
- Add corn, bamboo shoots, or bean sprouts
