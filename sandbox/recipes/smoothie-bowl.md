# Tropical Smoothie Bowl

**Prep Time:** 5 minutes
**Servings:** 1

## Ingredients

### Base:
- 1 frozen banana
- ½ cup frozen mango chunks
- ½ cup frozen pineapple chunks
- ½ cup coconut milk or almond milk
- 1 tbsp honey (optional)

### Toppings:
- Granola
- Fresh berries (strawberries, blueberries)
- Coconut flakes
- Chia seeds
- Sliced banana
- Almond butter drizzle

## Instructions
1. Add all base ingredients to a high-speed blender
2. Blend until smooth and thick (add more liquid if needed)
3. Pour into a bowl
4. Arrange toppings in an aesthetically pleasing pattern
5. Serve immediately

## Tips
- Smoothie should be thick enough to eat with a spoon
- Use less liquid than you would for a drinking smoothie
- Frozen fruit is key for the right consistency
- Add protein powder for a post-workout meal

## Variations
- **Berry Bowl:** Use mixed berries instead of tropical fruit
- **Green Bowl:** Add spinach and use banana and avocado
- **Chocolate Bowl:** Add cacao powder and peanut butter
