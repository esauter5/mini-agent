# Honey Garlic Salmon

This is one of my favorite easy and delicious salmon recipes. The sweet and savory honey garlic glaze pairs perfectly with the fresh salmon.

## Ingredients
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts 
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a small bowl, mix together the garlic, honey, soy sauce, lemon juice, and ginger. 
3. Season the boneless, skinless chicken breasts with salt and pepper. 
4. Place the salmon in a baking dish and brush the honey garlic sauce over the top.
5. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.
6. Serve immediately, drizzling any extra sauce over the top.

Enjoy this easy, flavorful salmon dish! The combination of honey, garlic, and ginger makes it so tasty.
