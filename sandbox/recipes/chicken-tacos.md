# Easy Chicken Tacos

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes
**Servings:** 6 tacos

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken:
- 1 lb chicken breast, diced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For Serving:
- 6 small corn or flour tortillas
- Shredded lettuce
- Diced tomatoes
- Shredded cheese
- Sour cream
- Lime wedges
- Fresh cilantro
- Salsa or hot sauce

## Instructions
1. Mix spices together in a small bowl
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
3. Add chicken and season with spice mixture
4. Cook for 8-10 minutes until chicken is cooked through and slightly charred
5. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave
6. Assemble tacos with chicken and desired toppings
7. Squeeze fresh lime over the top
8. Serve immediately

## Variations
- Add black beans and corn for extra filling
- Use ground turkey or beef instead of chicken
- Make it spicy with jalapeños or chipotle peppers
